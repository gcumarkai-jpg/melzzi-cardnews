Static, flyaway hair indoors? ⚡

Heated rooms keep the air dry, and wool hats and sweaters rub against your hair all day, so static builds up fast.
Try three small habits starting tonight:
Run a humidifier · Smooth a drop of oil on the ends · Swap to a wide-tooth comb
